# PCS Business Rules

## Rule 001

Every Organization owns one or more Plants.

---

## Rule 002

Every Plant owns one or more Projects.

---

## Rule 003

Every Project owns one WBS Tree.

---

## Rule 004

Every Activity belongs to one WBS.

---

## Rule 005

Every User belongs to one Organization.

---

## Rule 006

A User may access multiple Projects.

---

## Rule 007

Every Transaction belongs to one Project.

---

## Rule 008

Every Transaction should be assigned to a WBS whenever possible.

---

## Rule 009

No Physical Delete.

Soft Delete only.

---

## Rule 010

Every Change must be Audited.

---

## Rule 011

Organization is detected automatically after Login.

---

## Rule 012

Users never see another Organization.
